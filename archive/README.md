# Archive: original course report (superseded)

`comparative_gdp_final_report.pdf` is the original report for this project, kept unchanged as a historical record. **It is superseded by [`report/comparative_gdp_report.md`](../report/comparative_gdp_report.md)**, which is generated from the reproducible analysis in `gdp/`.

Why it was superseded: applying the report's own method (quarter-over-quarter log growth of seasonally adjusted real GDP) to its stated source doesn't reproduce its headline numbers. For example, it gives mean growth of about 0.6% per quarter for the US/Canada average, where the PDF reports 4.48%, and no significant regional gap, where the PDF reports a persistent gap of about 3.9 pp. The original input file wasn't kept, so the cause can't be confirmed. The most likely cause is a differently defined input series. See the section *Relation to the original report* in the current report.

The PDF also labels the series "North America" and "Europe". The current report calls them the "US/Canada average" (an unweighted mean of the two countries' growth rates) and "Germany", which is what they are.

Don't cite the PDF's quantitative results.
